Overview

This privacy policy explains how Northgate Software Ltd. processes personal data when you visit this website.

Data we collect

When you visit this website, our server logs your IP address, the page requested, and the time of the request. These logs are retained for 7 days for security and operational purposes, then deleted automatically.

If you use the contact form, we collect your name, email address, and the content of your message to respond to your enquiry. We do not share this data with third parties.

Analytics

We use Plausible Analytics, a privacy-friendly analytics tool that does not use cookies and does not collect personal data. No consent is required for analytics on this website.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. Contact us at hello@example.com.
