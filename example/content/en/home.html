We focus on backend systems that need to run reliably for years — APIs, data pipelines, developer tooling, and operational platforms. We don't do quick launches and hand-offs. We stay involved.

Opinionated architecture

We choose boring, proven technology. We avoid framework churn and dependency bloat. Every architectural decision has a reason we can explain.

Full stack ownership

We build it, we operate it, we on-call for it. No hand-offs to teams who weren't in the room when the decisions were made.

Long-term thinking

Systems that are easy to understand in three years are worth more than systems that are impressive today. Readability and operability are features.

Backend development

REST and gRPC APIs, event-driven systems, data pipelines, and background workers. Go, Python, and Rust.

Developer tooling

CLI tools, build systems, deployment automation, and internal platforms. Tools your team will actually use.

Architecture & review

System design, architecture reviews, and technical due diligence. An outside perspective on the decisions that matter.
